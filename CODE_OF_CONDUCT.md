# Code of Conduct

## The short version

This is a community of people trying to get the mathematics right. That sets the tone:

- **Be rigorous about ideas, kind to people.** Tear into a proof; never into a person.
  Strict review of a contribution is the point of this project — strict treatment of
  a contributor is not.
- **Assume good faith.** Assume the other person is trying to get it right and may
  know something you don't.
- **Credit generously.** Cite, acknowledge, and celebrate others' contributions.
- **Keep it on-topic and welcoming** to newcomers, students, and people from outside
  economics. A naive question is a chance to improve the docs, not to condescend.

## Not welcome

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, or demeaning language of any kind;
sustained disruption; publishing others' private information. Passing off others' work
as your own. Using the project's spaces (issues, discussions, PRs) for off-topic
promotion or spam.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — the repository, issues, pull requests,
discussions — and to public spaces when you are representing the project.

## Enforcement

Reports go privately to **Moran Koren — korenmor@bgu.ac.il**. Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer may warn, remove contributions, or block accounts for
behaviour that violates this code, in proportion to the harm.
